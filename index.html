PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT

Automated findings are evidence, not an accession certificate. A valid BagIt package can still lack authority, provenance or sufficient context.

Five tests: correspondence · interpretability · authenticity support · authority · integrity. The rules follow the supplied “Package quality: five tests and acceptance decision” text.
